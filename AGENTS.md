- Keep viability as a BDI 18%–0% area range; persist 18% only as the conservative legacy database value, because users no longer choose BDI.
- Sale mode means building with SBPE financing in the CPF of whoever finances (investor partner or the builder) and selling the finished house to any buyer. The price is the expected market price (`precoVenda`, optional helper: price per m² of finished homes × planned area). The seller pays brokerage, 15% IR on the capital gain (progressive above R$ 5 million; cost basis per IN SRF 84/2001 art. 17: lot price paid, ITBI and registry, construction with approved project including the builder remuneration, documented expenses and interest paid; reduction factor FR2 of Lei 11.196/2005 weighted by the month of each cost, lot counted from signing as a conservative estimate) and the "Na venda" expenses (lien release and certificates); the buyer pays ITBI and registry of the sale. Legacy records without `precoVenda` use the break-even price.
- Builder remuneration = margin (or global value) + process administration + projects + technical fees (`lucro`, `administracao`, `projetos`, `honorarios`; detail persisted as `remuneracaoDetalhe`). All of it comes out of the construction budget and reduces the viable area the same way in both modes: the house is the same whether it is built to live in or to sell. The user may fill only the global value or each part; itemized parts appear as separate lines in the reports, the margin stays inside "Construção da casa". Projects, administration and fees are never expenses. Legacy sale records stored the sale profit in `lucro_desejado`, so they load with margin 0.
- Use labor plus materials per m² as the real build cost for area and sale calculations; keep published CUB and CUB+10% as non-binding comparison only. Persist new simulation assumptions inside resultado JSON so legacy records remain readable without schema changes.
- Post-construction installments are cash outflows, not wholly interest expense; show estimated financing charges separately and label investor return assumptions instead of asserting bank-exact totals.
- Persist entry, FGTS intended for use, lot equity, annual compound-equivalent interest and PCI monthly releases in resultado JSON; keep the legacy monthly rate column monthly so old simulations can be edited without migration.
- Apply PCI releases only to financing remaining after lot payoff; preset 18 months over 320 m² and treat the table as editable planning assumptions rather than lender guarantees.
- Do not deduct a fixed contingency reserve: viable area uses all operational resources; old simulations may still carry `contingencia` inside resultado and are shown without it.
- Registry fees (compra e venda on lot value, alienação fiduciária on financed value) come from the official 2026 per-UF bracket tables in `src/lib/emolumentos.ts` (all 27 UFs; sources from the RI Digital/ONR collection or the state court), totals include the state funds and seal the table shows but not ISS or prenotação; states with a separate guarantee table (AP, TO, PR's FUNREJUS rule) use it for the alienação fiduciária. Where the court table omits surcharges, they are added from the state rule: RS digital seal + processing, MT table F, PR FADEP 5% + FUNREJUS 0,2% (cap) + estimated seal; SE's TJ guide already includes funds and seal. An unknown UF falls back to a labeled 0,5% estimate. The SFH first-home flag halves both (Art. 290, Lei 6.015/73) and is persisted in resultado as `primeiroImovelSfh`.
- The result page hides every zero-valued item. In morar mode it is a lay client proposal: area that fits, total cost with lot, client's own money, then "Como o imóvel é pago" (bank funds actually used = lot payoff + build financing, FGTS, lot equity, own money, minus unspent bank budget) summing exactly to the total cost; builder details (contract, costs, schedule, releases) sit in a collapsed section. Sale mode leads with area, expected sale price, total cost and the builder's profit after the cascade, and has a second tab with the investor report (`?visao=investidor`) when there is a partner.
- Down payment is derived from the contract, not typed: inputs mirror the Caixa simulator in BRL (valor do imóvel, valor do financiamento; valor de entrada = imóvel - financiamento, read-only), no percentages, filled first by lot equity (ágio), then FGTS (morar only: FGTS is for the holder's own home, so sale mode forces it to zero, hides the field with an explanation and zeroes it when switching to vender), then cash ("dinheiro necessário"). Equity and FGTS never become extra construction money; the construction budget is operation value - lot value, and releases for the build are capped by it. Legacy `entradaDinheiro` is ignored; legacy records without `valorOperacao` default to financed / 80%. The entry is proof of funds, not a payment to the bank; to live in (morar) its cash part is applied to the build, while for sale it stays in the account unless the agency requires the deposit (see sale-mode investor capital).
- Construction money pays construction, extras and "Durante a obra" expenses only. Pre-contract and signing expenses, cash entry and monthly construction interest are the client's own outlay (`desembolso*`), shown separately; FGTS never pays fees or interest. FGTS in the entry is debited at signing and released with the measured stages (Manual FGTS Moradia Própria 8.5.2), so it funds the build but is not start cash: both modes need 10% of construction + extras as start cash (`capitalGiro`), returned with the releases; to live in it is reduced by cash entry left after completing the lot, for sale only when the entry is deposited (`entradaDepositada`). `dinheiroParaComecar` = cash needed up to the first measurement (pre-contract + signing outlay + start cash; for sale also the lot part the bank does not pay and a required deposit). The budget label is "Verba disponível para obra". Sale-mode investor capital is described in its own rule below.
- For sale, the lot is part of the total cost ("Custo total (terreno + obra)") and of the settlement: the operation profit is the sale cash after brokerage and the bank debt, minus the capital returned (which includes the lot), so the lot is never counted twice nor added to the price.
- Building permit estimate: R$ 2,00 per m² + R$ 52,00; permit and INSS estimates follow the planned area (viable area until it is set).
- Lot origin: investidor (own lot; equity counts in the entry and as investor capital; no ITBI/purchase registry when paid off), construtor or compra (lot bought in the operation: no equity, ITBI and registry due, full price paid to the seller at signing; the builder receives it).
- Sale split is a cascade: return investor capital, then a preferred return at net Selic (regressive IR by term) + premium, then split the surplus by the investor share. Investor return is an annualized IRR compared with net Selic; Selic comes from the BCB SGS series 432 (editable).
- Own lot enters capital at the value typed; its appreciation over the price paid ("Quanto foi pago pelo lote (para o IR)", blank = no gain) is taxed at the sale. Settlement is cash-based: sale - brokerage - IR - sale expenses - bank debt + unspent financing = capital returned + investor share + builder share, exactly. Reference prices: break-even (returns capital) and minimum (capital + preferred return), solved by bisection because IR is not linear. Scenarios: expected, 10% lower price, same price 6 months later. Partner switch (`temInvestidor`: "Investidor parceiro" / "O próprio construtor"); with a partner the builder's share of the sale profit defaults to 0% (`participacaoInvestidor` 100), and the preferred return field appears only when the builder takes a share. Builder gain = remuneration + share of the sale profit (`ganhoConstrutor`).
- Construction-phase charges follow the Caixa booklet (Cartilha Juros Fase de Obras): debt = bank-paid lot portion from signing + PCI releases at the end of each measured month; each month AM = debt x TR and interest = (debt + AM) x monthly rate (effective annual -> nominal/12), plus MIP/DFI insurance and fee (BRL/month, from the Caixa simulator). No amortization until the build is finished and registered. TR comes from BCB SGS series 226 (editable). Post-build charges use the full debt (lot + build) and exclude TR: TR applies only during the construction phase.
- Lot payoff by the bank = outstanding lot balance (or purchase price), capped at 30% of the contract value (accepted without a separate lot appraisal) and by the financed amount; any remainder is covered by the cash entry. There is no lot-financing percentage input. The lot value typed is the contract value (no appreciation). The report suggests contract values: when the lot payoff exceeds 80% of the lot value, lot = payoff / 0.8 and construction = property value - lot (paper reallocation only; property value and total financing unchanged). This is never a user input.
- Wizard order: 1 starting point, 2 budget, 3 viability (planned area set here), 4 bank schedule (last, suggested term follows the planned area). The lot is part of the total cost in both modes: the label is "Custo total (terreno + obra)" with the lot/build split.
- Results have two views for build-to-live (morar) (sale mode has the investor report instead, see below): the builder view (full detail; builder remuneration is a cost line and part of the property cost so payment sources close) and the client proposal (?visao=cliente, components/app/PropostaCliente.tsx): light printable sheet (window.print / PDF); the margin stays inside "Construção da casa" and itemized administration, projects and technical fees appear as their own cost lines. Bank amount shown to the client excludes unused build money (folga). Infographics: traffic light (red if first post-build installment > 30% of income, yellow if planned area needs extra cash), house filled by payment sources, area ruler, cash timeline, installment vs income. The post-build installment follows the Caixa simulator inputs (sistemaAmortizacao PRICE|SAC, default PRICE; prazoFinanciamento, default 420 months) on the full debt, without TR (parcelasAmortizacao, prestacaoInicial).
- Public proposal link (`/proposta/$token`, no login, noindex): token = simulation id + expiry (30 days) + HMAC-SHA256 signed with the server service role key (`src/lib/proposta-token.ts`); a server function reads the simulation with the admin client and returns only the client proposal built by `montarProposta` (`src/lib/proposta.ts`) plus the builder's name, WhatsApp and professional registry (CREA/CAU), never the raw resultado or e-mail. Builder WhatsApp and registry live in Supabase auth `user_metadata` (`telefone`, `registro`, edited on the profile page; no migration) and drive the "Quero seguir com essa proposta" wa.me button. Morar simulations link to the client proposal; vender simulations with a partner and the current calculation link to the investor report (`montarRelatorioInvestidor`, `src/lib/relatorio-investidor.ts`, shown in the `?visao=investidor` tab and on the same public route with a "Quero participar" CTA). RLS stays closed.
- Step 1 warns (non-blocking) when the financed amount is lower than the down payment: values were probably swapped with the Caixa simulator.
- Income pre-analysis (`/pre-analise`, `src/lib/pre-analise.ts`) is an estimate, never "approval": installment ≤ 30% of gross family income (formal + informal), term = min(420, 80 years 6 months - age of the oldest), MCMV bands from the CCFGTS rules of 24/03/2026 (up to 3.200/5.000/9.600/13.000; property limits 270k/270k/400k/600k; reference nominal rates at the band top 4,5/6,5/7,66/10%, -0,5 pp for FGTS holders with 3+ years in bands 1–3; above band 4 SBPE at an estimated 11,5%). Property value = min(income: max(capacity / 0.8, capacity + own lot), entry resources / 0.2, band limit); financing = min(80% of property, property - own lot). Program rates are nominal (monthly = annual / 12); the effective equivalent is handed to the simulator, whose rate field is effective. "Levar para uma nova simulação" prefills the wizard through search params (renda, credito, valorImovel, fgts, terreno, lote, juros, prazoFin, sistema, seguro); subsidies are not modeled.
- Simulation cards (`components/app/ResumoSimulacao.tsx`) show mode, UF, last update, planned area (or viable range) and, without cents: morar = proposal total cost, own money, installment and % of income plus the proposal traffic light (shared `resumoProposta` from PropostaCliente, so card and report always agree); vender = expected sale price, builder profit after the cascade and the cash the investor needs (`capitalMaximo`); records without `capitalMaximo` (older calculation) show sale, total cost and profit plus "Abra e salve de novo". Legacy records show only what they have. The app theme is always dark (`:root` is dark, no `.dark` class), so use light shades directly instead of `dark:` variants.
- Wizard inputs that change the result never come pre-filled: state (UF, which drives registry fees and the CUB), finish standard, effective interest and MIP/DFI + fee start empty and are required in step 2 ("Copie do simulador da Caixa"); save re-validates steps 1–3. Changing steps scrolls to the top of the form and focuses the title; step errors render as an alert next to the buttons and scroll into view. The wizard keeps a browser draft (`localStorage` key `sinal-verde:rascunho:<id|nova>`) once the user types, offers "Continuar de onde parei"/"Descartar", warns before closing the tab and clears the draft on save; mark it dirty outside the input event (setTimeout), because a state update in the capture phase dropped the first typed character.
- The client's own money is shown by destination (`bolsoDetalhe` in the proposal): cash entry (part of the property value, applied to the build), lot complement, documents and fees, construction interest and extra for the planned area. Fees are never paid out of the entry: the entry belongs to the property value, so using it for fees would leave the build short.
- Dashboard cards: click the name to rename (only `nome` is updated), duplicate a simulation as "(cópia)" to compare scenarios, search by name (accent-insensitive) when there are more than 3, and records saved by an older calculation show "Abra e salve de novo".
- Pre-analysis translates the property value into size with the average price per m² of a finished home in the region (lot + construction, from listings; `precoM2Pronto`), never the municipal "valor venal"; the exact build area comes from the full simulation.
- Sale-mode investor capital follows real costs, not the contract: at signing, pre-build and signing expenses, the lot part the bank does not pay and 10% start cash (returned at the end of the build); each measured month, construction interest plus the real build cost beyond the build financing (pro rata to the releases); then installments until the sale. The cash entry is proof of funds by default; with `entradaDepositada` (agency requires the deposit) it is paid at signing, covers the start cash and the cost beyond financing, and the unused part returns at the end of the build. `capitalMaximo` (peak simultaneous exposure) is "o investidor precisa ter disponível"; `capitalAportadoInvestidor` is the total applied over time.
- Investor report (components/app/RelatorioInvestidor.tsx): light printable sheet with only the investor's side, never the builder's margin or internal costs: traffic light (red if the expected IRR is below net Selic, yellow if the 10%-lower or 6-month-delay scenario falls below it), cash needed (`capitalMaximo`, which excludes the investor's own lot because it is not cash to set aside; the lot is shown separately), what comes back at the sale, IRR bars against the agreed minimum and net Selic, a timeline (signing, build, until sale, sale), the cascade rule, the scenarios, how much the price can fall before losing the minimum return or money, and the risks (financing in the investor's name, installments if the sale is late).
- The live summary in the wizard has a traffic light (`sinalDaSimulacao` in finance.ts): morar = red above 30% of income, yellow when the planned area needs extra cash, green otherwise; vender = red when the expected return is below net Selic (or a loss), yellow when the 10%-lower or 6-month-delay scenario falls below it, green otherwise; grey until costs or the sale price are filled in. The sale block shows "Para onde vai o dinheiro da venda" (brokerage, IR, sale expenses, bank payoff, capital back, profit split) and the investor report repeats it as a stacked bar, with notes on the R$ 440 mil single-property and 180-day reinvestment exemptions and on PF-to-company equivalence for more than two units (RIR/2018, art. 162).

- Keep viability as a BDI 18%–0% area range; persist 18% only as the conservative legacy database value, because users no longer choose BDI.
- For sale simulations, derive the asking price as `(total costs + desired profit in BRL) / (1 - brokerage rate)`, because profit is a currency target and brokerage must be covered by the sale.
- Reserve the builder remuneration from buildable credit only when building to live in (morar); for sale, profit comes from the sale price and never reduces the viable area. Add profit only once to the sale price; honoraria and administration flow through the expense breakdown so they are never added again to total costs.
- Use labor plus materials per m² as the real build cost for area and sale calculations; keep published CUB and CUB+10% as non-binding comparison only. Persist new simulation assumptions inside resultado JSON so legacy records remain readable without schema changes.
- Post-construction installments are cash outflows, not wholly interest expense; show estimated financing charges separately and label investor return assumptions instead of asserting bank-exact totals.
- Persist entry, FGTS intended for use, lot equity, annual compound-equivalent interest and PCI monthly releases in resultado JSON; keep the legacy monthly rate column monthly so old simulations can be edited without migration.
- Apply PCI releases only to financing remaining after lot payoff; preset 18 months over 320 m² and treat the table as editable planning assumptions rather than lender guarantees.
- Do not deduct a fixed contingency reserve: viable area uses all operational resources; old simulations may still carry `contingencia` inside resultado and are shown without it.
- Registry fees (compra e venda on lot value, alienação fiduciária on financed value) come from the official 2026 per-UF bracket tables in `src/lib/emolumentos.ts` (all 27 UFs; sources from the RI Digital/ONR collection or the state court), totals include the state funds and seal the table shows but not ISS or prenotação; states with a separate guarantee table (AP, TO, PR's FUNREJUS rule) use it for the alienação fiduciária. Where the court table omits surcharges, they are added from the state rule: RS digital seal + processing, MT table F, PR FADEP 5% + FUNREJUS 0,2% (cap) + estimated seal; SE's TJ guide already includes funds and seal. An unknown UF falls back to a labeled 0,5% estimate. The SFH first-home flag halves both (Art. 290, Lei 6.015/73) and is persisted in resultado as `primeiroImovelSfh`.
- The result page hides every zero-valued item. In morar mode it is a lay client proposal: area that fits, total cost with lot, client's own money, then "Como o imóvel é pago" (bank funds actually used = lot payoff + build financing, FGTS, lot equity, own money, minus unspent bank budget) summing exactly to the total cost; builder details (contract, costs, schedule, releases) sit in a collapsed section. Sale mode leads with area, sale, total cost and profit.
- Down payment is derived from the contract, not typed: inputs mirror the Caixa simulator in BRL (valor do imóvel, valor do financiamento; valor de entrada = imóvel - financiamento, read-only), no percentages, filled first by lot equity (ágio), then FGTS (morar only: FGTS is for the holder's own home, so sale mode forces it to zero, hides the field with an explanation and zeroes it when switching to vender), then cash ("dinheiro necessário"). Equity and FGTS never become extra construction money; the construction budget is operation value - lot value, and releases for the build are capped by it. Legacy `entradaDinheiro` is ignored; legacy records without `valorOperacao` default to financed / 80%. The entry is proof of funds, not a payment to the bank; its cash part is applied to the build.
- Construction money pays construction, extras and "Durante a obra" expenses only. Pre-contract and signing expenses, cash entry and monthly construction interest are the client's own outlay (`desembolso*`), shown separately; FGTS never pays fees or interest. FGTS in the entry is debited at signing and released with the measured stages (Manual FGTS Moradia Própria 8.5.2), so it funds the build but is not start cash: both modes need 10% of construction + extras as start cash (`capitalGiro`), reduced only by cash entry left after completing the lot, returned with the releases; `dinheiroParaComecar` = pre-contract + signing outlay + start cash, shown separately from the permanent client outlay. The budget label is "Verba disponível para obra". Investor (the borrower who lends their credit) capital is a dated cash-flow list: own-lot equity, FGTS and cash in the entry, pre-build expenses, 10% start working capital (net of entry cash only, returned at the end of the build), monthly construction interest and installments until the sale.
- For sale, the lot value is part of the cost base: sale price = (costs + lot + profit) / (1 - brokerage), and scenario results subtract the lot too.
- Building permit estimate: R$ 2,00 per m² + R$ 52,00; permit and INSS estimates follow the planned area (viable area until it is set).
- Lot origin: investidor (own lot; equity counts in the entry and as investor capital; no ITBI/purchase registry when paid off), construtor or compra (lot bought in the operation: no equity, ITBI and registry due, full price paid to the seller at signing; the builder receives it).
- Sale split is a cascade: return investor capital, then a preferred return at net Selic (regressive IR by term) + premium, then split the surplus by the investor share. Investor return is an annualized IRR compared with net Selic; Selic comes from the BCB SGS series 432 (editable).
- Own lot enters investor capital at its net liquidation value: equity minus brokerage (default 5%) and 15% IR on the gain over the purchase price (optional input). For sale, the user enters the builder desired profit; total profit = post-build charges + preferred return + both surplus shares, solved by fixed-point iteration, and it drives the sale price. There is no separate own-resources field: planned area above the viable one generates the client aporte.
- Construction-phase charges follow the Caixa booklet (Cartilha Juros Fase de Obras): debt = bank-paid lot portion from signing + PCI releases at the end of each measured month; each month AM = debt x TR and interest = (debt + AM) x monthly rate (effective annual -> nominal/12), plus MIP/DFI insurance and fee (BRL/month, from the Caixa simulator). No amortization until the build is finished and registered. TR comes from BCB SGS series 226 (editable). Post-build charges use the full debt (lot + build) and exclude TR: TR applies only during the construction phase.
- Lot payoff by the bank = outstanding lot balance (or purchase price), capped at 30% of the contract value (accepted without a separate lot appraisal) and by the financed amount; any remainder is covered by the cash entry. There is no lot-financing percentage input. The lot value typed is the contract value (no appreciation). The report suggests contract values: when the lot payoff exceeds 80% of the lot value, lot = payoff / 0.8 and construction = property value - lot (paper reallocation only; property value and total financing unchanged). This is never a user input.
- Wizard order: 1 starting point, 2 budget, 3 viability (planned area set here), 4 bank schedule (last, suggested term follows the planned area). The lot is part of the total cost in both modes: the label is "Custo total (terreno + obra)" with the lot/build split.
- Results have two views for build-to-live (morar): the builder view (full detail; builder remuneration is a cost line and part of the property cost so payment sources close) and the client proposal (?visao=cliente, components/app/PropostaCliente.tsx): light printable sheet (window.print / PDF), never shows margin, honoraria, administration or follow-up fees; those are grouped into "Construção da casa". Bank amount shown to the client excludes unused build money (folga). Infographics: traffic light (red if first post-build installment > 30% of income, yellow if planned area needs extra cash), house filled by payment sources, area ruler, cash timeline, installment vs income. The post-build installment follows the Caixa simulator inputs (sistemaAmortizacao PRICE|SAC, default PRICE; prazoFinanciamento, default 420 months) on the full debt, without TR (parcelasAmortizacao, prestacaoInicial).
- Public proposal link (`/proposta/$token`, no login, noindex): token = simulation id + expiry (30 days) + HMAC-SHA256 signed with the server service role key (`src/lib/proposta-token.ts`); a server function reads the simulation with the admin client and returns only the client proposal built by `montarProposta` (`src/lib/proposta.ts`) plus the builder's name, WhatsApp and professional registry (CREA/CAU), never the raw resultado or e-mail. Builder WhatsApp and registry live in Supabase auth `user_metadata` (`telefone`, `registro`, edited on the profile page; no migration) and drive the "Quero seguir com essa proposta" wa.me button. Only morar simulations get a link; RLS stays closed.
- Step 1 warns (non-blocking) when the financed amount is lower than the down payment: values were probably swapped with the Caixa simulator.
- Income pre-analysis (`/pre-analise`, `src/lib/pre-analise.ts`) is an estimate, never "approval": installment ≤ 30% of gross family income (formal + informal), term = min(420, 80 years 6 months - age of the oldest), MCMV bands from the CCFGTS rules of 24/03/2026 (up to 3.200/5.000/9.600/13.000; property limits 270k/270k/400k/600k; reference nominal rates at the band top 4,5/6,5/7,66/10%, -0,5 pp for FGTS holders with 3+ years in bands 1–3; above band 4 SBPE at an estimated 11,5%). Property value = min(income: max(capacity / 0.8, capacity + own lot), entry resources / 0.2, band limit); financing = min(80% of property, property - own lot). Program rates are nominal (monthly = annual / 12); the effective equivalent is handed to the simulator, whose rate field is effective. "Levar para uma nova simulação" prefills the wizard through search params (renda, credito, valorImovel, fgts, terreno, lote, juros, prazoFin, sistema, seguro); subsidies are not modeled.
- Simulation cards (`components/app/ResumoSimulacao.tsx`) show mode, UF, last update, planned area (or viable range) and, without cents: morar = proposal total cost, own money, installment and % of income plus the proposal traffic light (shared `resumoProposta` from PropostaCliente, so card and report always agree); vender = estimated sale, total cost, profit. Legacy records show only what they have. The app theme is always dark (`:root` is dark, no `.dark` class), so use light shades directly instead of `dark:` variants.
